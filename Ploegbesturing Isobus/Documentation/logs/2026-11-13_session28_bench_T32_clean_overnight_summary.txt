One figure that moved, and why: the longest loop pass
-----------------------------------------------------

  2026-11-13 23:22:50   Loop pass: longest 18152 us since boot
  2026-11-14 08:26:53   Loop pass: longest 121897 us since boot

Cause: the logger hand-over at 23:26:36, not the firmware under test. The L1 logger was stopped and the
overnight logger started a few seconds later, with the 1 Hz line on. While no program on the PC has the
port open, a print on a Teensy 3 waits for the PC until the core's transmit timeout (TX_TIMEOUT_MSEC 70 in
cores/teensy3/usb_serial.c; it is a calibrated loop count, measured here as 122 ms) and then drops the
text. The log shows it: the first lines after 23:26:36 are cut off in the middle and three lines are
missing.

Reproduced on 2026-11-14:
  08:29:15  dump: longest 2706 us since the last dump (the normal case, 2 minutes with two menu prints)
  08:29:43  logger stopped, port closed for 6 s, new logger started at 08:29:50
  08:29:58  dump: longest 121903 us since the last dump

So: one stall of about 122 ms each time a PC is connected over USB with the port closed and the firmware
prints. After that one timeout the core drops prints at once until the PC reads again. With no PC on the
USB port (the tractor) a print returns at once. The PC-side pause at 02:12:19 (one line arrived a second
late, nothing lost) left no trace on the board.
